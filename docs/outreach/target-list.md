# Target list — who to approach first

**Status: draft, for the owner's judgment. Nobody has been contacted. This is a
prioritized reading of the existing 60-artist survey (`assets/js/data.js`), not new
research — every fact below is already published on the artist's own page on this site.
No claim here goes beyond what's already in `data.js`.**

M2's target is 10 lots from 5 artists the owner did not create. This list is longer than
5 on purpose — expect most outreach to go unanswered, so the funnel needs more than 5
names at the top.

Selection logic: artists who (a) already have the most written about them here (`count`
in `data.js` — more coverage means a warmer, more specific opener), and (b) have an
obvious, low-effort first lot implied by what's already been written about them, so the
ask in the pitch isn't abstract. Ranked, not exhaustive — the remaining ~45 are fallback,
not excluded.

---

## Tier 1 — warmest opener, clearest first lot

1. **Otis McDonald** (`otis-mcdonald`) — 8 tracks here, the most of anyone, and the pitch
   writes itself: "the most-heard musician you have never heard of." Beatmaker — a
   plausible first lot is a stem/sample pack from one of the eight tracks already named
   on his page, or a signed print of one. Bay Area, no stated label — likely reachable
   directly.

2. **Rob Araujo** (`rob-araujo`) — 6 tracks, soloist/keys. His whole identity here is
   "voicings other players would build a record around, then he cuts them off." A
   handwritten chart or a short unreleased take fits that identity exactly — the lot
   copy is already implied by the artist copy.

3. **DJ Harrison** (`dj-harrison`) — 4 tracks, runs the Jellowstone studio (stated on his
   page), plays keys in Butcher Brown. Physical studio ephemera (a tape reel, a test
   pressing) fits a working studio owner better than most beatmakers on this list. Also
   a bridge to Butcher Brown (below) through one relationship.

4. **Reuben James** (`reuben-james`) — 4 tracks, known for very short interludes (one is
   69 seconds). A bespoke short commission — a 60-second interlude made to order — is a
   lot type this artist's own style suggests, not one invented for him.

5. **Kiefer** (`kiefer`) — 2 tracks but real name recognition (Stones Throw, Tiny Desk
   already cited on his page). "Diaristic" is the word already used to describe him — a
   handwritten page or a demo sketch matches that description directly.

## Tier 2 — strong hook, slightly colder or more coordination needed

6. **Jazzbois** (`jazzbois`) — 5 tracks, a full band (Budapest), which means more people
   to coordinate but also more possible lots (stage-used gear from any member).

7. **Athletic Progression** (`athletic-progression`) — Nordic trio who name tracks after
   basketball moves — a distinctive enough gimmick that merch tied to that naming
   convention could be a genuinely fun lot, not just "a used cymbal."

8. **Vooo** (`vooo`) — no face, no interviews, on-page mystique — a harder ask (may not
   want to be found at all) but if reachable, the anonymity itself is the pitch: a rare
   named/attributed release, unusual for someone who deliberately stays faceless.

9. **GoGo Penguin** (`gogo-penguin`) — signed to Blue Note, likely has management rather
   than a personal inbox. Bigger name, bigger credibility if it lands, but the pitch
   probably needs to go through a label/booking contact, not a DM.

10. **Makaya McCraven** (`makaya-mccraven`) — only 1 track here but the biggest name on
    the list by reputation ("beat scientist," cut-up method already described on his
    page). Worth one attempt precisely because a single well-known name lends the whole
    store credibility for everyone approached after.

## Notes for the owner

- Tiers reflect **warmth of opener and lot clarity**, not musical importance — several
  count:1 artists (Yussef Dayes, Kamaal Williams) are bigger names but a thinner written
  relationship to lean on, so they're deliberately not in the first wave.
- Bands and collectives (Jazzbois, Athletic Progression, Okvsho, Waaju, Kokoroko, PYJÆN)
  need one point of contact willing to speak for the group — flag that in the first
  message rather than assuming.
- None of these have been contacted. This file is a queue, not a commitment.
