## What shipped

- **`docs/outreach/seller-pitch.md`** — a short DM-length pitch and a longer follow-up
  version, plus objection handling for the owner's reference. States plainly: no fee, no
  card payment yet, settlement arranged by hand between seller and winner (per M1's
  explicit scope — no card payments, no entity, no take-rate). Never promises a future
  cut number.
- **`docs/outreach/target-list.md`** — 10 artists from the existing 60-artist survey,
  ranked by how much is already written about them (`count` in `assets/js/data.js`) and
  how obvious a first lot is given what's already public about their work. Every claim
  in it traces to `assets/js/data.js`, cited inline — no new research, no invented
  facts. Explicitly notes bigger names (Yussef Dayes, Kamaal Williams) were left out of
  the first wave for having a thinner *written* relationship, not for being less
  important.
- **`docs/outreach/lot-copy-guide.md`** — five questions to ask a real seller, a copy
  pattern modeled on the existing artist-blurb voice in `data.js` (since no real-lot
  precedent exists yet), one clearly-labeled fictional example to show the pattern, and
  guardrails: never invent an answer, never write the seller's quote for them, never
  imply a photo exists if it doesn't (ties to `MISSION.md`'s no-faked-photography rule).
