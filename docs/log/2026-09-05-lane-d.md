# Lane D — 2026-09-05

## What happened

Three PRs (#12, #13, #14) were open simultaneously and **all three conflicted**. The owner
merged #11 and found the rest broken. There was no merge order that would have worked.

## Cause

`PROGRESS.md` and `docs/BACKLOG.md` were writable by every lane. Four shifts a day rewriting
two shared files means every open PR conflicts with every other one. An earlier version of
`docs/LANES.md` explicitly said conflicts there were "cheap" — they are the most expensive
kind, because they block the queue.

Secondary symptom of the same cause: an `A7` id collision between two branches, both
inventing the next free number against a base that had already moved.

## Fixed

- `PROGRESS.md`, `docs/BACKLOG.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/LANES.md` are **single-writer, Lane D
  only**. Every other shift writes `docs/log/YYYY-MM-DD-lane-<x>.md` — unique filename,
  cannot conflict.
- `tools/shift/preflight.mjs` enforces it: rebases onto the newest `origin/main`, refuses
  cross-lane edits, refuses a missing log. Mandatory before pushing.
- #12, #13 and #14 folded in here, keeping every item and renumbering Lane C's `A7`→`A10`,
  `A7b`→`A10b`, `A8`→`A11`, `A9`→`A12` to clear the collision.

## Still open for the owner

  instead. The browser lane has no browser until "Run now" pre-approves it.
- Analytics is approved but unbuilt, and is now the critical path for E1.
