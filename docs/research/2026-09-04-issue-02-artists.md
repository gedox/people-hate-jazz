# Issue 02 artist research — first pass

**Lane D, 2026-09-04.** First research note in this repo (`docs/research/` was empty
before this file), so nothing to rotate away from yet. Topic: **artists for Issue 02**
— feeds `docs/BACKLOG.md` item B4 (Issue 02 longlist) directly.

Scope: recent (last ~8 months, mostly 2026) releases in modern jazz, jazz fusion,
jazz-adjacent underground hip-hop, and neo-soul. Sourced from Bandcamp Daily, All About
Jazz, Jazz Weekly, label sites, and artist Bandcamp pages via web search — I have no
browser and did not listen to any of this, so treat "why it fits" as a pitch to verify,
not a review. `daily.bandcamp.com` and `chaoticgoodjazz.substack.com` were blocked by
this session's network egress proxy, so their picks are represented only via search
snippets, not read directly — worth a full pass by a shift that isn't blocked.

Picked for genuine under-coverage over name recognition. One candidate (Shane Sato)
credits an artist already in the roster (Reuben James), which could make a nice
cross-reference if we run it.

### Dave Adewumi — *The Flame Beneath the Silence*
**Label:** Giant Step Arts · **City:** Brooklyn, NY (recorded live at Ornithology)
**Link:** https://daveadewumimusic.bandcamp.com/album/flame-beneath-the-silence

Debut as a leader, released March 27, 2026. Post-bop trumpet date with a serious rhythm
section — Joel Ross (vibraphone), Linda May Han Oh (bass), Marcus Gilmore (drums) — recorded
live off the floor. Reviewed by All About Jazz, Jazz Weekly, Making a Scene. A strong,
straightforwardly excellent debut that hasn't crossed into the outlets our readers already
follow — exactly the under-covered case B4 asks for.

### Lolivone de la Rosa — *Jewels*
**Label:** appears self-released/independent — no label found in search, confirm before writing
copy · **City:** New York, NY (born in Puerto Rico, NYC-based since 2021)
**Link:** https://lolivone.bandcamp.com/album/jewels

Debut album, recorded Nov 2025 at East Side Sound, NYC. Guitarist working bebop and Afro
Puerto Rican rhythm against each other — Charlie Parker and Cheo Feliciano covers sit next to
originals. "One foot in Puerto Rico, one in NYC; one foot in jazz's past, one in its present"
per the Bandcamp Daily blurb. A debut, a woman leading on guitar (still rare in jazz coverage),
and a bicultural angle — fits the under-covered brief well.

### Dolphin Hyperspace — *Echolocation*
**Label:** self-released (Bandcamp) · **City:** Los Angeles, CA
**Link:** https://dolphinhyperspace.bandcamp.com/album/echolocation

Released May 1, 2026. Saxophonist Nicole McCabe and bassist Logan Kane, a free-jazz-fusion
duo out of the LA DIY/beat scene — largely improvisational, electronics and rock bleeding into
the jazz. Guests include Louis Cole (KNOWER), Gerald Clayton, and Justin Brown (Thundercat,
Ambrose Akinmusire). Self-released, no PR machine behind it — genuinely underground rather than
just adjacent to it, and it's the kind of maximalist, beat-culture jazz this magazine's whole
premise says it should cover.

### Atlantis Jazz Ensemble — *Mystic Suite*
**Label:** Marlow Records · **City:** Ottawa, Canada
**Link:** https://atlantisjazzensemble.bandcamp.com/album/mystic-suite

Released Feb 27, 2026, recorded live to a 1965 tube 2-track. Third album in a trilogy
(*Oceanic Suite* 2016, *Celestial Suite* 2023, *Mystic Suite* 2026) from an Ottawa spiritual-jazz
quintet explicitly working the Strata-East/Tribe/Black Jazz lineage. Reviewed by PAN M 360 and a
couple of smaller jazz blogs, nothing bigger. A working band with a real track record rather
than a one-off session — could anchor a feature rather than a blurb.

### Shane Sato — *Wavelength*
**Label:** Mixto Records · **City:** Los Angeles, CA
**Link:** https://www.willwork4funk.com/lofi-jazz-neo-soul-in-shane-satos-sophomore-album-wavelength

Released May 15, 2026. Japanese-American multi-instrumentalist's sophomore album — lo-fi jazz,
indie R&B, neo-soul. Features Braxton Cook and, notably, **Reuben James** — already three
tracks deep in our own tracklist (*Ruby Smiles*, *Vegan Butter — Intro*, *Rice & Peas —
Interlude*). If Issue 02 wants a piece that explicitly builds on Issue 01 rather than starting
cold, this is the one with a built-in bridge.

### _BY.ALEXANDER — *Memories for Sale —at—> 66 Greene St Soho NY (Deluxe)*
**Label:** independent (Bandcamp) · **City:** New York, NY (Soho)
**Link:** https://byalexander.bandcamp.com/album/memories-for-sale-at-66-greene-st-soho-ny-deluxe-edition

Deluxe edition released July 17, 2026, written through grief after the artist's father died.
Jazz sound palette pushed through pop structure and chopped, club-ready production; the deluxe
edition adds Ghostface Killah and Rapsody. The named features raise its visibility risk — it's
the least "undiscovered" pick here — but the base album is small and independent, and the
jazz/club hybrid is a genre lane we don't have anyone else in yet.

### Eligh x FAZE.ONE — *The Way the Light Looks*
**Label:** self-released, Eligh Music (Bandcamp) · **City:** Bay Area / LA (Living Legends crew)
**Link:** https://eligh.bandcamp.com/album/the-way-the-light-looks

Released June 4, 2026. 18 tracks, jazz-inflected boom bap over warm lo-fi and electronic
texture. Eligh's been underground hip-hop since the Living Legends in the '90s — a veteran, but
one whose new work doesn't get covered as jazz-adjacent even though this record leans hard that
way. Good contrast piece against the younger names above.
