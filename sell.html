The Store — for artists

List something.

One object, or one block of your time. Verify who you are, describe the lot plainly, and it goes into a queue a human reads before it ever goes live — it does not appear in the store the moment you hit submit.

  • Reviewed, not automatic. A person checks every lot before it's live.
  • No payment collected here. If it sells, you and the bidder settle directly.
  • No password. A one-time code sent to your email is how we know it's you.
  • Nothing faked. No photo? We generate a plate from the category — never a stock image.

Who's listing?

We email a six-digit code to attribute this lot to you and let you come back to it later. Nothing is stored except the address and the code's expiry.

What are you listing?

Write it the way you'd tell a friend what it is and why it's real. Vague copy gets sent back by the reviewer — specific copy is what makes a stranger trust a bid.

Photo

No upload here on purpose — this prototype never presents a fake product photo. Every lot renders a generated two-ink plate keyed to its category instead. A photo field arrives once the queue is real.

It's in the queue.

Your lot is pending, not live. A reviewer reads it, sets a close date, and either approves it into the catalogue or sends it back with a note. Nobody can bid on it until that happens.

If it sells

How settlement works

At close, the highest bid wins and both of you get the other's contact details by email. You arrange payment and shipping directly — this site takes no cut and holds no money, because it isn't a company yet.

If it goes wrong

There's no escrow and no chargeback here — that's the honest trade for there being no fee. If a buyer disappears, tell us; a lot with a non-paying winner is remade available rather than left in limbo. This is early, and it says so everywhere on purpose.

Who sees your email

Nobody, until a lot actually closes with a winner. It's never shown on the listing, never sold, never used for anything but the one code above and, if you win or sell, one introduction.
