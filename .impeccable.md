# Design Context — PEOPLE HATE JAZZ

## What this is
An online music magazine covering modern jazz and jazz-adjacent hip-hop, built around
a 100-track playlist ("Jamies") and its 60 artists. Editorial in the lineage of
Pitchfork / Rolling Stone / Bandcamp Daily, but with the visual attitude of a
risograph zine.

## Audience
Gen Z and younger millennial crate-diggers. People who follow labels, not charts.
They already know what a Tiny Desk is. They are bored by streaming-service UI.

## Brand personality
Opinionated, dry, a little smug. Print-first. Loud typography, quiet color.
Never twee, never "lofi beats to study to."

## Aesthetic direction — NEW WAVE / RISOGRAPH / AVANT GARDE
- **Newsprint as substrate.** Bone paper ground, real ink black, visible grain.
- **Risograph accents.** Fluorescent flame orange + ultramarine. Colors that look
  mis-registered on purpose. Overprint, not gradient.
- **Brutalist editorial grid.** Asymmetric. Hairline rules. Hard left alignment.
  Big numerals. Things allowed to touch the edge of the page.
- **Type does the work.** Oversized condensed display, editorial serif for reading,
  monospace for metadata and kickers.

## Typography
- Display / masthead: **Bricolage Grotesque** (variable width + optical size)
- Reading: **Newsreader** (editorial serif, optical sizes)
- Metadata / kickers / UI: **Space Mono**
No Inter. No Roboto. No Poppins.

## Color
- paper  #E8E2D4   ink    #14110E
- flame  #FF3B0F   (riso fluorescent orange — the accent)
- ultra  #1B2ECC   (riso ultramarine — the second accent)
- Dark mode inverts to ink ground with bone type; accents stay.

## Hard NOs (AI slop test)
- No cyan-on-dark. No purple/violet gradients. No glassmorphism.
- No generic rounded card grids with drop shadows.
- No emoji as UI. No "✨". No centered hero with a gradient blob.
- No Inter/Roboto/Poppins/Montserrat.

## Technical constraints
- Static site. No build step. No framework.
- 60 YouTube embeds → must use click-to-play facades, never 60 live iframes.
- Must be keyboard navigable and respect prefers-reduced-motion.

---

## Second half: THE STORE (auction)

The site is two departments of one publication, switched in the top bar.

- **Magazine** (`index.html`) — the editorial survey. Accent: **flame orange**.
- **Store** (`store.html`) — the auction. Accent: **ultramarine**, with flame
  demoted to a warning colour (closing soon, outbid). The accent swap is the
  primary signal of which half you are in.

Store routes are hash-based (`#/`, `#/closing`, `#/shops`, `#/shop/<slug>`,
`#/lot/<id>`, `#/mybids`, `#/how`).

### Rules that must hold
- **No photography exists** for any lot. Never fake a product shot. Each lot
  renders a generated two-ink **plate** whose drawing is chosen by category
  (rings for vinyl, ruled staves for paper, waveform for tape, knobs for gear,
  halftone for photo, diagonals for wearable, two circles for one-on-one time).
  Art is deterministic per lot id, so it never reshuffles between builds.
- **The prototype banner stays.** Lots are invented and the artists are real;
  the page must say so, plainly, above the fold.
- **Catalogue paginates at 24.** All 140 at once is a 64,000px page with 140
  inline SVGs and it stalls the renderer.
- Bidding state lives in `localStorage` under `phj-store-v1`. The auction clock
  is anchored to `pinned` (first visit) so countdowns survive reloads.
