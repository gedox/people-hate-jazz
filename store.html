The Store

The other half of this publication. Sixty artists from the survey, each with a private shop, each selling things that only exist once — a test pressing, a page out of a notebook, the cymbal that cracked, two hours of somebody's undivided attention.

How bidding works

  • 1. Bids must beat the current bid by at least the step shown.
  • 2. Steps rise with price: $10 under $200, up to $250 over $2,500.
  • 3. Highest bid at close wins. There are no proxy bids.
  • 4. One of everything. Nothing here gets restocked.

Status

This is a working front end, not a live marketplace. The catalogue is invented, the artists are real, and nothing on this page constitutes an actual offer to sell. Wire it to a server and it becomes one.

Your bids and watchlist live in localStorage. Clearing site data clears them. Nothing is sent anywhere.
