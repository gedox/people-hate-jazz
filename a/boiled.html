“Four minutes of restraint. Get in early.”

An Italian outfit with an exclamation mark in the name and a few hundred views per upload, making some of the most assured post-jazz on this list. 'Interi Ora' is four minutes of restraint from a band that could clearly show off and elects not to. Get in early.
