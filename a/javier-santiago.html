Javier Santiago

SOLOIST/Minneapolis / Bay Area/ropeadope · keys · spiritual/2 cuts

“Lets the flute say the part the piano can't.”

A pianist who treats the ancestor question literally. 'Ancestor's Blues' brings in Wonway Posibul and Elena Pinderhughes and then lets the flute say the part the piano can't. Ropeadope's most underrated signing, and one of the few artists here with two takes on the same idea in one playlist.
