Redtenbacher's Funkestra

BAND/London, UK/jazz-funk · big band · bass-led/1 cut

“The long version is better and they know it.”

Bassist Stefan Redtenbacher runs the tightest jazz-funk big band in London and puts most of it on YouTube for nothing. 'Squid Exit Music' with Mike Outram on guitar is the seven-inch edit; the seven-minute version is better and they know it.
